News Topic Summariser

Enter a news topic. The tool searches public news sources using Bing News, retrieves articles from reputable outlets across the political spectrum, and uses Azure AI to produce a balanced summary of key perspectives.

! Warning Source perspective labels are approximate. Always read the original articles before drawing conclusions.
For example: UK general election, climate change policy, NHS funding
